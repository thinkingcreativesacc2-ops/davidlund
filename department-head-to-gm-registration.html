The Career Reality

Why the Move From Department Head to GM Is a Financial Leap, Not Just a Title Change

Most hotel professionals are promoted for their operational skill — then discover, in their first ownership meeting, that the GM role runs on an entirely different currency: financial fluency.

What Got You Here

Department Head Thinking

  • Running a tight, well-managed department
  • Hitting your own budget line and labor targets
  • Delivering guest satisfaction and team performance
  • Reporting numbers up the chain when asked
What Gets You There

General Manager Thinking

  • Owning the full hotel P&L, not just one department
  • Explaining and defending performance to ownership
  • Thinking in profitability and flow-through, not just topline
  • Leading every department head with financial clarity
