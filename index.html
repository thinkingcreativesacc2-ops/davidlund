Meet Your Instructor

David Lund, The Hotel Financial Coach

For more than two decades, David Lund has worked at the intersection of hotel operations and hotel finance — first as a controller and financial leader inside hotel operations, then as an educator, keynote speaker, and author who has made it his mission to remove the fear and confusion from hotel financial statements.

David built The Hotel Financial Coach because he watched too many talented operators — General Managers, Department Heads, and rising leaders — stall in their careers not because they couldn't run a great hotel, but because they couldn't confidently read, explain, or defend a P&L in front of ownership. His teaching is different from a traditional accounting course: no jargon, no theory for theory's sake — just the practical financial skills hotel leaders actually use, taught the way hotel leaders actually think.

"Every hotel leader deserves to walk into the P&L meeting with confidence — not dread."

David is the author of The Prosperous Hotelier and a sought-after keynote speaker at hospitality conferences, ownership meetings, and leadership summits across North America.
