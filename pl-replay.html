How to Read Your P&L in 30 Minutes

Originally Presented: Wednesday, July 22, 2026 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM EST

Most hotel leaders were never actually taught how to read a P&L — they pieced it together on the job and hoped it was enough. That gap shows up at the worst possible moments: budget meetings, ownership calls, and every time your numbers get put on the table.

In this replay, David Lund walks you through a real hotel P&L line by line — showing you exactly where to look first, what each number is really telling you, and how to walk away with a clear, confident read in 30 minutes or less.
