Meet Your Instructor

David Lund

Hotel Financial Leadership Expert Keynote Speaker Author Founder, The Hotel Financial Coach

David Lund has spent his career at the intersection of hotel operations and hotel finance — and he built The Hotel Financial Coach around a simple belief: hospitality leaders don't get passed over because they lack talent. They get passed over because no one ever taught them how to speak the language of ownership with confidence.

Through his writing, keynote talks, and coaching, David has helped thousands of hotel professionals become stronger, more confident financial leaders — turning P&L statements from a source of anxiety into one of their greatest career assets.

His approach is practical, direct, and built for people who run hotels, not spreadsheets — which is exactly why leaders at every level, from department head to owner, trust him to help them lead with clarity.
